Turn your workouts into a weekly drink bank.

Earn Those Beers is an iPhone app that reads the active calories you burn from Apple Health and shows them as drinks in a weekly bank. Workouts count in full; everyday movement counts for less. Log what you drink and watch the balance.

  • Weekly bank that resets on the day you choose
  • Beer styles and sizes, wine, shots, cocktails, or your own drink
  • Daily and weekly limits you set, counted in standard drinks, with gentle warnings
  • One-tap ride buttons: don't drink and drive
  • Home Screen widget

Your data stays on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

For adults of legal drinking age only. Earn Those Beers is a calorie-tracking game, not medical or health advice. No amount of alcohol is safe, and exercise does not cancel out its effects.
