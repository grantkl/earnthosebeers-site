Support

Need help or found a bug? Email earnthosebeerssupport@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

My bank shows 0 or isn't updating

Check that the app can read Apple Health: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Earn Those Beers, and turn on Active Energy and Workouts. Calories from a watch or fitness tracker appear once they sync to Apple Health. Pull down on the History tab to refresh.

How are drinks earned?

Workout calories count in full. Other active calories (everyday movement) count at a lower weight you can change in Settings. One banked drink equals the calories of a standard beer. The bank resets each week on the day you choose.

What's a standard drink?

14 g of alcohol: about 12 oz of beer at 5%, 5 oz of wine at 12%, or 1.5 oz of spirits at 40%. Your daily and weekly limits count standard drinks, so a 20 oz IPA counts for more than a light beer.

Can I edit or delete a drink?

Yes, for the current week. In History, tap a drink to change it or delete it, or swipe left to delete.

Why does the app ask my birthdate?

To confirm you're of legal drinking age where you live. The birthdate stays on your iPhone.

Important

Earn Those Beers is a calorie-tracking game. It is not medical, health, nutritional or professional advice. No amount of alcohol is safe, and exercise does not cancel out alcohol's effects on your body. Calorie numbers are estimates, and "earned" drinks are not a recommendation or permission to drink. Never drink and drive.

If you're worried about your drinking, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and available 24/7 in English and Spanish: 1-800-662-4357. See also NIAAA Rethinking Drinking.
