Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: Earn Those Beers keeps your data on your iPhone. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads your active energy (active calories) and workouts from Apple Health to calculate your weekly drink bank. It never writes to Apple Health. Health data is read and used only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used for anything other than showing you your bank. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

What's stored on your device

  • Drinks you log (type, size, calories, standard drinks and time)
  • Your settings (week start, limits, earning weight)
  • Your birthdate, used only to check that you're of legal drinking age
  • A summary of your bank, shared with the app's Home Screen widget on the same device

This data stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple manages under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Tips

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app only remembers on your device that you've tipped, to say thanks.

Links to other apps and sites

Ride buttons open Uber or Lyft, and the resources section links to health organizations. Those services have their own privacy policies; we don't share anything with them.

Children

The app is for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children. It asks for a birthdate and blocks use below the legal drinking age for your region.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
